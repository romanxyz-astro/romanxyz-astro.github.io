RomanXYZ

Mapping the three-dimensional Universe with Roman grism spectroscopy

A community-developed toolkit for Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope WFI grism data: from count-rate images to calibrated spectra, redshifts, emission-line maps and kinematic stamps for every source in the field. An open-source release is coming soon.

Background: a simulated Roman G150 grism exposure. Every streak is the spectrum of one galaxy or star. (Simulation: Wang et al. 2022)

What is RomanXYZ?

The Roman Wide Field Instrument carries a slitless G150 grism covering 1.00–1.93 μm at a wavelength-dependent resolving power of R = 461 λ/μm (about 460–890 across the band). Every exposure records the spectrum of every source on 18 detectors at once, with no target pre-selection. This spectroscopy is a core component of the High-Latitude Wide-Area Survey, which will measure redshifts from Hα (z = 0.5–1.9), [O III] (z = 1.0–2.9) and [O II] (z = 1.7–4.2) over thousands of square degrees.

RomanXYZ reduces these data. It starts from count-rate (L2) images and produces source catalogs, flux-calibrated 2-D and 1-D spectra with exact error and covariance propagation, contamination models, emission-line redshifts, emission-line maps, and per-exposure kinematic stamps for rotation-curve and kinematic-lensing studies. The step sequence, products and command-line interface follow the JWST/NIRCam slitless reduction developed for the SAPPHIRES survey, adapted to Roman. Traces and sensitivities come from the roman_disperser optical model.

One detector (SCA 9) of the Wang et al. (2022) simulation through the pipeline: 8,025 sources → 5,924 spectra → 2,735 with an emission line at S/N ≥ 5.

The pipeline

One command, romanxyz reduce, runs the full chain from rate images to science products. Every step writes a status table and can be re-run on its own. The pipeline is tested on simulations with known truth.

Data Access

RomanXYZ aims to provide openly accessible and reproducible scientific data products.

Users of publicly released RomanXYZ products are requested to cite the corresponding data release and associated publications. Specific usage terms and citation instructions will accompany each release. Non-public data are not distributed through this website.
